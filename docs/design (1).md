# Brand Identity & Design System: Genius Library (जीनियस लाईब्रेरी)

## 1. Brand Overview
**Genius Library** is an institutional digital repository blending traditional cultural values (*Genius*) with modern digital education and literature. The emblem features Goddess Saraswati seated on a lotus with a Veena and sacred texts, surrounded by a classic circular seal with Devnagari and English typography.

---

## 2. Color Palette
The color scheme is derived directly from the divine seal, combining deep midnight blue (representing wisdom, infinity, and digital depth) with warm gold and parchment tones (representing heritage, enlightenment, and sacred literature).

## 3. Typography Guidelines

### Primary Typefaces
* **Devnagari Script (Titles & Brand Name):** `Mangal`, `Poppins`, or traditional Devnagari serif equivalents for a majestic and authentic cultural feel.
* **English Typography (Headings & Body):** 
  * *Headings:* **Playfair Display** or **Cinzel** (Serif for elegance and editorial prestige).
  * *Body Copy:* **Inter** or **Roboto** (Clean Sans-Serif for maximum legibility in digital reading interfaces).

### Hierarchy & Scale
* **H1 (Document Title):** 32px / Bold / Midnight Navy (`#1E2635`)
* **H2 (Section Header):** 24px / Semi-Bold / Terracotta Bronze (`#947359`)
* **H3 (Subsection Header):** 18px / Medium / Charcoal Slate (`#4B4344`)
* **Body Text:** 15px / Regular / Line-height 1.6 / Charcoal Slate (`#4B4344`)

---

## 4. Logo Usage & Emblem Guidelines
* **Emblem Structure:** Circular seal containing Goddess Saraswati, Lotus, Holy Books, and Swan, bordered by dual concentric golden rings.
* **Bilingual Typography:** Features "जीनियस लाईब्रेरी" in Devnagari script along the lower arc, balanced with "THE GENIUS DIGITAL LIBRARY" styling where applicable.
* **Clear Space:** Maintain a minimum padding equal to 25% of the emblem diameter around all digital placements.
* **Background Contrast:** Use on ivory/off-white (`#F3F3EE`) or deep midnight navy (`#1E2635`) backgrounds to preserve contrast and sacred aesthetic integrity.

---

## 5. UI / UX Application & UI Components
* **Buttons & CTAs:** Primary buttons in Deep Midnight Navy (`#1E2635`) with Warm Parchment Gold (`#BFA486`) text or hover state.
* **Cards & Containers:** Light Ivory background (`#F3F3EE`) with subtle Warm Parchment borders (`#DED0BC`).
* **Icons & Dividers:** Rich Terracotta Bronze (`#947359`) for iconography and decorative horizontal rules.
